In Europe, the Iberian lynx and the Mediterranean monk seal are two species teetering on the edge of extinction. The lynx, once widespread across the Iberian Peninsula, now survives in fragmented pockets due to habitat loss and declining rabbit populations, its main prey. Meanwhile, the monk seal—one of the world’s rarest marine mammals—faces threats from fishing gear entanglement and coastal development. Conservationists are restoring scrubland for lynx and establishing marine reserves for seals, while public awareness campaigns aim to shift attitudes and protect these fragile lives. By supporting habitat restoration, sustainable tourism, and wildlife advocacy, we become part of their survival story. Europe’s wild heart still beats—let’s make sure it’s heard.
