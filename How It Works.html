Endangered species aren't scattered randomly—they’re rooted in unique ecosystems shaped by geography, climate, and human impact. In this website we will be spotlighting two species from each continent. By doing so, we connect users not only to the animals themselves but to the global diversity they represent. This structure makes it easier to understand how threats like deforestation, pollution, and climate change play out differently around the world. Just as the Arctic’s melting sea ice endangers Emperor Penguins, habitat fragmentation in Italy threatens the Marsican Brown Bear. Each continent tells a distinct story—and by giving space to these voices, we show that conservation is not one-size-fits-all. It’s local, complex, and urgent. This approach also fosters a stronger emotional connection. You can discover how species in your own region are doing, and feel inspired to protect life not just “out there,” as many say, but right at home.
